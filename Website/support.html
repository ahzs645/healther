Before You File

Please include the export source, file format, the fields you are trying to map, and whether you are using a built-in template or manual import. Do not attach private health exports publicly. If needed, share a small synthetic sample with personal values removed.

Privacy

Healther processes imports locally on your device. Review the Privacy Policy for HealthKit and local data details.
