The whole flow

Three steps. No magic.

The same flow whether it's a one-off file or your monthly export routine — only the second step gets shorter once you save a template.

01

Pick a file

Choose a CSV, JSON or TSV from anywhere on your device. healther parses the headers and shows you exactly what's inside.

// Fitbit_Export.csv
date,steps,bpm,sleep✓
2024-05-12,8643,72,7.4✓
2024-05-13,9117,68,7.1✓
2024-05-14,7902,71,—!
02

Map columns — once

Connect each column to its Health field, set units and date format, then run it once or save the mapping as a reusable template.

date_utc avg_hr step_count
startDate heartRate stepCount
03

Import to Health

Records are written to Apple Health through HealthKit, on your device only. Every batch is reversible — one click undoes it.
