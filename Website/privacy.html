Healther

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Summary

Healther is a local-first app for importing user-selected CSV and JSON health exports into Apple Health. The app does not require an account, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not send your health data to us or to a third-party server.

Data You Choose To Import

When you select a CSV or JSON file, Healther reads the file on your device so you can preview and map fields to Apple Health types. The selected file contents are processed locally. Healther does not upload these files or their contents.

Apple Health Access

Healther asks for HealthKit permission so it can save compatible records to Apple Health when you choose to import them. HealthKit access is controlled by iOS. You can review, change, or revoke Healther's Health access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Data Stored On Your Device

Healther may store custom import templates, import history summaries, and source metrics locally on your device using iOS app storage. This local data is used to show recent imports and reusable mappings. It is not transmitted to us.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal information, health data, diagnostics, identifiers, usage analytics, or tracking data from Healther.

Sharing Templates

If you export or share a template, the exported file contains mapping configuration such as field names, Health field selections, date keys, and format settings. Review exported templates before sharing them if your source field names are sensitive.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open an issue at github.com/ahzs645/healther/issues.
